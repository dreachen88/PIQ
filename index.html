04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity.
From the start.

Where should we start?+

Choose one workflow, a recurring bottleneck, or a question your team struggles to answer. Use the project brief below to capture the opportunity.

Can this fit our existing tools?+

That is a discovery question. The right approach depends on your systems, available APIs, data permissions, and operating needs. Define those dependencies before setting a build scope.

Do we need a fully formed idea?+

No. A clear business problem is a useful starting point. Outline who experiences it, how it works today, and what a better outcome would look like.

What about timelines and cost?+

Both depend on the scope, integrations, and data readiness. A focused brief helps establish requirements before making delivery or commercial commitments.
